Paper 1 Scope (60M)
RIDDLE #1 Topic 9, slide 21

The "BLUE" Status Trap

Multicollinearity: OLS is STILL BLUE! Variances are large, but among all linear unbiased estimators, OLS still has the minimum variance.

Hetero & Auto: OLS is linear and unbiased, but LOSES the "B" (inefficient, tests biased).
MNEMONIC Topic 5, slide 4

4 Error Term Reasons (O.R.M.I.)

  • O: Omission of variables (parsimony & data limits).
  • R: Randomness in human behaviour (unpredictability).
  • M: Measurement error in surveys/data.
  • I: Imperfect functional form (linear proxy).
MNEMONIC Topic 11, slide 9

4 DW Assumptions (I.N.A.N.)

  • I: Intercept must be present in regression.
  • N: Non-stochastic X explanatory variables.
  • A: AR(1) scheme only: \( u_t = \rho u_{t-1} + v_t \).
  • N: NO lagged Y regressor (use Durbin h or LM test!).
CRITICAL DISTINCTION Topic 9, slide 17

Omitted vs Irrelevant Variable

Omitted Relevant: BIASED & INCONSISTENT! Destroys all estimators. Lethal error.
Irrelevant Included: UNBIASED & CONSISTENT, but INEFFICIENT (larger variances).
2-PART FORMULA Topic 8, slide 10

Interaction Terms (D × X)

\( Y = \beta_0 + \beta_1 X + \beta_2 D + \beta_3 (D \times X) \)

1. Intercept Shift (\(\beta_2\)): Difference in baseline when \( X=0 \).
2. Slope Shift (\(\beta_3\)): Extra marginal return per unit of \( X \).
Total Effect: \( \beta_1 + \beta_3 \) for category \( D=1 \).
PROCEDURE Topic 10, slide 10

White Test & Robust SEs

1. Auxiliary Reg: Regress \( e_i^2 \) on \( X \), \( X^2 \), and cross-products \( X_j X_k \).
2. Test Stat: \( n \cdot R^2 \sim \chi^2_{df} \). \( H_0 \): Homoscedasticity.
3. Remedy: White's heteroscedasticity-consistent SEs (ls(h)). Coefficients unchanged; SEs adjusted!

STEP-BY-STEP REPS

Hands-On EViews & Microsoft Word Sequence

Follow these exact commands inside EViews and Word to secure 40/40 marks.

1 Inspect Data & Group Descriptive Stats

Open econ_325_data.wf1. Hold Ctrl, select Y, X1, X2, right click → Open → as Group. Click View → Descriptive Statistics → Individual Samples.

Exam Rep: Copy stats table into Word. Comment on whether Mean > Median (right-skewed) or Mean < Median (left-skewed).
2 Estimate Equation & Name Model

Click Quick → Estimate Equation. Enter: y c x1 x2. Click OK. In equation window, click Name → enter TABLE01.

Exam Rep: Freeze table (Freeze), copy to Word. State ( H_0: eta_j = 0 ) and interpret with "Holding other variables constant".
3 White Heteroscedasticity Test

In equation window: View → Residual Diagnostics → Heteroskedasticity Tests → select White → leave cross-terms unchecked.

Exam Rep: Read Obs*R-squared. State ( H_0 ): Homoscedasticity. Since ( p = 0.1017 > 0.05 ), do not reject ( H_0 ).
4 Huber-White Robust Standard Errors

In equation window: click Estimate → Options tab → under Coefficient covariance matrix select Huber-White → OK.

Exam Rep: Note in Word that point coefficients do NOT change; only standard errors and t-statistics are corrected!
5 Estimate Log-Log Model (Elasticities)

Click Quick → Estimate Equation → type: log(y) c log(x1) log(x2). Click OK.

Exam Rep: Interpret ( eta_1 ) as constant elasticity: "A 1% increase in X1 leads to a 0.0855% increase in Y on average."
6 Save Word File: SURNAME_INITIALS.docx

Save your Microsoft Word file as SURNAME_INITIALS.docx in the exam folder. Never use personal names on public shared versions.

Exam Rep: Verify document has all tables named (TABLE01, TABLE02) and graphs (GRAPH01) with written answers directly below.
