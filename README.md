## 🏛️ What's Inside

1. **🗺️ 8-Node Econometrics Mastery Tree**:
   - Bite-sized 60-second micro-challenge cards covering CLRM disturbance term assumptions, Multicollinearity & VIF, Heteroscedasticity & White's test, Autocorrelation & Durbin-Watson, Model Specification Bias & Ramsey RESET, Dummy Variable Traps, EViews workflows, and NWU 4-part interpretation standards.

2. **⚡ Interactive In-App EViews Guided Dojo**:
   - Real EViews menu click-path sequences (`Quick → Estimate Equation`, `View → Residual Diagnostics → White Test`, `Options → Huber-White / HAC Newey-West`).
   - Live simulated regression output table with 1-click injection into the Word answer generator.

3. **📝 NWU 4-Part Word Answer Generator**:
   - Automatically formats statistical write-ups into the NWU 4-step marking standard:
     1. Hypotheses ($H_0: \beta_1 = 0 \text{ vs } H_1: \beta_1 \ne 0$)
     2. Computed test statistic & $p$-value
     3. Decision rule ($p < 0.05 \implies \text{Reject } H_0$)
     4. Economic interpretation with the mandatory phrase: *"holding other variables constant"*.

4. **🏛️ 26-Mark Written Theory Pillars**:
   - The recurring 5-step diagnostic framework: Assumption, Nature of Violation, Detection Methods, Consequences for OLS, and Remedies for Multicollinearity, Heteroscedasticity, Autocorrelation, and Specification Bias.

5. **🧭 Durbin-Watson 5-Zone Radar**:
   - Live slider calculating $\hat{\rho} \approx 1 - \frac{d}{2}$ and highlighting the exact decision zones ($d < d_L$, $d_L \le d \le d_U$, $d_U < d < 4-d_U$, etc.).

6. **📊 Real Past Paper Decoders (2021–2025)**:
   - Deconstructed exam questions including Beef Demand ($n=10$), Charity Donations ($n=65$), and Comrades Marathon ($n=150$) with missing $t$-statistics ($t = b / \text{se}$) and $F \leftrightarrow R^2$ relations.

7. **⚔️ Active Recall Quiz Atelier**:
   - 20 high-yield True/False past exam questions with instant, reassuring explanations.

8. **🎧 40Hz Audio Focus Synthesizer**:
   - Pure Web Audio binaural beat generator ($136.1\text{ Hz} \leftrightarrow 176.1\text{ Hz}$) to anchor concentration during study sessions.
