TRAP #3 • 2 MARKS FORFEITED Silent Garbage

STDEV.S(Mean, Median) Summary Stat Disaster

What happened: You entered =STDEV.S(B50, C50) passing the mean and median as observations! Excel returned 37,165 (the SD of two numbers) instead of the true 92,119 raw sample SD!

The Fix: STDEV.S strictly requires an array of raw data, NEVER summary averages.

Raw Sample SD Formula

The Fix: Never hardcode row numbers on categorical data. Use conditional aggregation functions.

Formula Pasted Instead of a Business Sentence

Slope Coefficient (b1)
t Stat = [Slope Coefficient] ÷ [Standard Error]

"Each additional R1 advertising is associated with an estimated R3.57 more monthly sales."

📐 Sandbox 2: Chapter 2 Descriptive Statistics (15 Marks)

Grouped Stats Lab & STDEV.S Disaster Demystifier

Compare the deadly summary stat mistake against correct array aggregation, and test continent distributions live.

✘ The Semester Test Disaster 0 Marks
=STDEV.S(MeanCell, MedianCell)

Returned: 37,165.41. This takes the standard deviation of exactly TWO numbers (the mean and median), producing completely meaningless silent garbage.

Hardcoded row slice =AVERAGE(C2:C98) was equally fatal: data was alphabetical by country, not continent!

✔ The Authentic Exam Recovery Full Marks
=STDEV.S(IF($B$2:$B$126="Africa", $C$2:$C$126))

Returned: 92,119.37 ($n = 29$). Calculated across all 29 raw country observations in Africa.

Mean: =AVERAGEIF($B$2:$B$126, "Africa", $C$2:$C$126) -> 62,786.59. No hardcoded row slices!
