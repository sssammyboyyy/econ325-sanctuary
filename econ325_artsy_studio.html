PAPER 1 GUARANTEED 26-MARK BLOCK

The 4 Diagnostic Theory Pillars

Every year, Question 1 or Question 4 asks the same 5-part architecture: Assumption, Nature of Violation, Detection Method, Consequences for OLS, and Remedies.

Multicollinearity Pillar

5 MARKS EACH
1. CLRM Assumption
No exact linear relationships among explanatory variables.
2. Nature of Violation
High imperfect linear correlation among regressors in the sample.
3. Detection Methods
1. High R² (> 0.8) with low individual t-statistics.
2. Pairwise correlations > 0.8.
3. Variance Inflation Factor: VIF > 10 (Tolerance < 0.10).
4. Consequences for OLS
OLS is STILL BLUE!
• Variances/SEs inflate.
• Confidence intervals widen.
• t-ratios turn insignificant.
5. Remedies (Do's and Don'ts)
1. Do nothing (often best).
2. Collect more data.
3. DO NOT drop an important variable, as this causes catastrophic omitted variable bias!
